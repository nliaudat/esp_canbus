# 3D Printed Enclosure — ESP32 CanBus Shield

Compact, two-part enclosure designed to house the ESP32 CanBus shield. 

Optimized for easy printing, only mounting screws required, minimal supports required. The lid is designed to be printed face down for best results.

> **⚠️ Important**
> The mounting screws must **not touch the PCB**. Even slight contact can cause shorts or permanent damage.  
> Apply **Kapton tape** under the screw heads to ensure proper clearance and reliable short‑circuit protection.

> **ℹ️ Info**
> If the PCB sits too loosely in the enclosure, use a small piece of paper as a shim.

## Recommended print settings

- Material: PLA or PETG
- Layer height: 0.2 mm
- Perimeters/shells: 2–4
- Base: Use organic supports for the RJ45 cutout
- Lid: Print face down for best results.
